Experiment 12: Digit Minimal-Pair Test (Results)

The decisive digit confound test: 111 matched pairs of text with and without digits, across 5 categories. Result: H6 is digit-independent (1.02× ratio). e164 is a digit detector (1,453× ratio). The two routing axes are orthogonal.

1. Design

111 matched pairs of text across 5 categories. Each pair shares the same source text; one version (with_digits) retains original numerals, the other (digit_stripped) has digits removed or replaced with word equivalents. This creates a minimal-pair design where digit density is the only systematic difference.

4. Key findings

  1. H6 is digit-independent (1.02× ratio). H6 fires at 134,252/M on digit-stripped prose and 136,915/M on prose with digits. The verse/prose axis genuinely detects text format, not digit density.
  2. e164 is confirmed as a digit detector (1,453× ratio). The original "scripture detector" finding was entirely a digit-density confound. Bible text has 0% digits (verse numbers stripped), all other corpora have digits.
  3. The two routing axes are orthogonal. H6 = verse/prose format (digit-independent). e164 = digit presence (format-independent). These are completely separate routing phenomena that were originally conflated in the e164 "scripture detector" narrative.
  4. Per-pair comparison: 81 pairs show with_digits > stripped, 24 show the reverse, 6 are equal. For H6, this asymmetry is not large enough to indicate a digit effect — the per-pair magnitude differences are small.
  5. Digit density correlation r = −0.22 for H6. Weak negative, no meaningful relationship. Compare to the original Exp 4 pilot where r = −0.642 (driven by KJV-file digit confound).

5. Implications

  1. The H6 verse/prose axis is NOT a digit artifact. This was the primary alternative explanation for the H6 finding. With 111 matched pairs, H6 shows a 1.02× ratio — digit density does not explain the verse/prose routing effect.
  2. e164 is definitively a digit detector. The 1,453× ratio across 5 categories, with perfect zero in 2 categories on digit-stripped text, is conclusive. The original "not-memorized-scripture" framing is fully retracted.
  3. Three independent tests now confirm H6: Exp 1 (translation-invariant, 3,415×), Exp 4b (fires on commentary prose not verse quotes, 163,284/M), Exp 12 (digit-independent, 1.02×). The H6 verse/prose axis is the strongest, most robust routing effect in the study.
  4. The digit confound is fully resolved. e164 = digits. H6 = verse/prose format. These are separate axes that require separate explanations. The digit question (H1) is answered: e164 is a digit detector, H6 is not.
