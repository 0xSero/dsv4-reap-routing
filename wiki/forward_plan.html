Forward Plan: Next Experimental Phase

Designed to resolve the H6 routing axis without recreating confounds. Designed by GPT-5.6-terra.

1. Rules

  • Read-only: no weight edits, no pruning, no quantization
  • Every experiment must control digit density
  • Fail-closed invariants on every record
  • External review before publication

2. Exp 13: Scoped H6 ablation

The causal test. Knock out H6 anchors, measure Δ-NLL. See Exp 13 page for full design.

3. Priority and dependencies

  1. Exp 13 (ablation) — highest priority, causal claim
  2. Exp 11 (context dump) — what triggers specialists
  3. Exp 3 (secular null) — religion-specific or general?
  4. Theology observation — Jesus/Lucifer/Moloch/Saturn

4. New experiments proposed

  • Format-edit factorial: same text, verse-formatted vs prose-formatted, religious vs secular — 2×2 design
  • Secular factorial: same format controls with no religious content at all
  • Lens robustness: does digit density affect J-space probes?

5. Visualization plan

  • Interactive H6 explorer (which experts fire on which passages)
  • Per-layer routing animations (how routing evolves through the 43-layer stack)
  • MSG vs KJV side-by-side (same passage, different format, different routing)

6. Narrative arc

The story: "We asked if an AI routes religious text differently. The answer is no — but we found something more interesting: a verse/prose format detector. Here's how we found it, how we ruled out confounds, and what it means."
