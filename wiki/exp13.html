Experiment 13: Scoped H6 Ablation

Causal intervention: knock out the 6 H6 anchor experts and measure Δ-NLL on prose vs verse. staged — hook deployed, corpus ready, not yet run

1. Rationale

Exp 1, 4b, and 12 establish that H6 correlates with verse/prose format. But correlation is not causation. Exp 13 asks: does H6 causally contribute to the model's processing of prose vs verse, or is it an epiphenomenon?

3. Intervention modes

  1. Contribution knockout: Keep gate selection (expert is still chosen) but zero its output. Measures the expert's direct contribution to the residual stream.
  2. Route-mask compensation: Prevent the expert from being selected; the gate reselects the next-best expert. Measures whether the model can compensate.

5. Primary outcome

Δ-NLL (change in negative log-likelihood) when H6 anchors are knocked out, compared verse vs prose. If H6 is causal, knocking it out should damage prose more than verse.

6. Controls

  • 20 matched control experts (randomly selected, matched for frequency and REAP rank)
  • Dose series: knockout 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 anchors
  • Secular verse/prose cells test whether the effect is religion-specific or format-general

7. Hook implementation

The ablation hook (exp13_ablation_hook.py, 14.6KB) is deployed to both TP2 nodes. It injects into the forward pass to zero out or mask H6 anchor experts. Read-only constraint respected: the hook modifies the forward pass during observation, not the model weights.
