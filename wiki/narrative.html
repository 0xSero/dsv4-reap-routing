Does DeepSeek-V4 route by religion?

An honest account, correctness-first. Written 15 Aug 2026, updated through Exp 1 expansion.

1. The question

We asked: does a sparse mixture-of-experts language model — DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, with 256 experts per layer and top-6 routing — distribute its computation differently when it reads religious text vs secular text? Does it have "Bible experts" or "Qur'an experts"?

2. The answer

No. The model does not route by religion. What we found instead is a large verse/prose format detector (H6) that fires on discursive prose and goes silent on verse-structured text — regardless of which religion, which translation, or which century the text is from.

3. The digit detector (e164)

Our original headline — expert e164 at L42 was a "not-memorized-scripture detector" — was wrong. Claude Opus 5's external review found the confound: our pipeline strips verse numbers from the Bible, making it 0% digits, while all other corpora contain digits. e164 fires on digits, not on "non-scripture." Exp 12 confirmed this definitively: 1,453× ratio between text with and without digits.

4. The verse/prose axis (H6)

What survives is H6: 13+ experts that fire at up to 163,284/M on prose and ~0 on verse. This is a format detector — it responds to whether text is lineated like verse or flowing like prose. It is:

  • Translation-invariant (Exp 1): KJV (1611) and ESV (2001) both fire at 0.0. The Message (a prose paraphrase) fires at 1,033/M — because it abandons verse format.
  • Digit-independent (Exp 12): 1.02× ratio with vs without digits.
  • Context-sensitive (Exp 4b): fires on commentary prose, not on verse quotes embedded within it.

5. No religion backbone

15/20 top L42 experts are shared between Bible and Christian corpora. All nine traditions share a common routing backbone. No tradition has a uniquely isolated routing profile.

6. Late prediction emergence

Logit-lens analysis shows the model's next-token predictions stabilize around layer 30–35 across all traditions. Earlier layers carry more input-level leverage (bounded Jacobian L0 norms are ~2.5–3.5× later layers). No tradition shows qualitatively different lens behavior.

7. What we got wrong (H3)

We hypothesized that effective-expert count inversely tracks text predictability (H3). This was wrong — the correlation is positive (+0.47), not negative. The 65 vs 111 gap was a window-length artifact. H3 is retracted.

8. Integrity chain

Every record passes fail-closed invariants (frequency sum = seqlen × topk, no NaN/Inf, correct shapes). Zero violations across all 3,682 records. The harness was verified line-for-line against the model's Gate.forward.
