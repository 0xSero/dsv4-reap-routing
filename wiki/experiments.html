Experiments

All experiments, numbered as in EXPERIMENTS.md. Each entry: hypothesis, design, status, result.

1. Current priority queue

  1. Exp 1 — 360 samples, 613,344 tokens. complete H6 = 59.5/M (excl. MSG), translation-invariant. MSG at 1,033/M confirms format detection.
  2. Exp 4b — 351 samples, 5,156,659 tokens. complete H6 = 163,284/M on commentary prose.
  3. Exp 12 — 222 samples, 437,900 tokens. complete H6 digit-independent (1.02×), e164 digit detector (1,453×).
  4. Exp 13 — Ablation hook designed, corpus staged (480 records). staged
  5. Exp 11 — Context dump. staged
  6. Exp 3 — Secular null. not staged

2. Exp 1 — Multi-translation Bible test complete

FieldValue
HypothesisDoes H6 fire on non-KJV renderings of the same text? (translation-invariance)
Design30 Bible passages × 12 English translations (5 public-domain + 7 copyrighted). All 0-digit controlled. 360 records, 613,344 tokens.
ResultTranslation-invariant for verse-formatted translations. H6 = 59.5/M (excl. MSG). KJV and ESV perfect zero. MSG (prose paraphrase) at 1,033/M confirms H6 detects format, not content. See Exp 1 results.

3. Exp 4b — Quotation-switch test complete

FieldValue
HypothesisDoes H6 fire on quoted verse, surrounding commentary, or both?
Design351 commentary windows with embedded KJV quotes, 18 sources, 5,156,659 tokens.
ResultH6 = 163,284/M on commentary prose. Fires on prose context, not verse quotes. r = −0.13 with quote fraction. See Exp 4 results.

4. Exp 12 — Digit minimal-pair test complete

FieldValue
HypothesisIs e164 a digit detector? Is H6 digit-independent?
Design111 matched pairs, 5 categories, 222 records, 437,900 tokens. Each pair: same text, one with_digits, one digit_stripped.
ResultH6 digit-independent (1.02× ratio). e164 digit detector (1,453× ratio). The two axes are orthogonal. See Exp 12 results.

5. Exp 13 — Scoped H6 ablation staged

FieldValue
HypothesisCausal: does knocking out H6 anchors damage prose more than verse?
Design4-cell corpus (verse/prose × religious/secular), 480 records. Ablation hook deployed to both TP2 nodes. Two modes: contribution knockout, route-mask compensation.
StatusHook designed and deployed. Corpus staged. Not yet run.

6. Exp 11 — Context dump staged

Re-run observation with --raw-budget-tokens to capture per-token routing. Extract top-500 firing contexts with ±20-token windows. Prediction: contexts are numerals, footnote markers, verse references. Needs GPU re-run.

7. Exp 3 — Secular null not staged

Is routing religion-specific or general-English? Compare religious vs matched secular text at same digit density. Staged after digit question settles.

8. Exp 8 — Sorted L42 frequency check done

Is e164 zero a permutation bug? No — 15/20 shared experts, ranks differ. The zero is real.

9. Exp 9 — Position/length confound answered

Is e164 zero a windowing artifact? No — Bible 0 / Qur'an 4,383/M at matched window sizes.
