2. Effective expert count

At layer 40: Bible 65, Christian 111 (aggregate). The aggregate is inflated by pooling; per-sample it collapses to 70. The ordering survives but it is not a predictability proxy (H3 retracted). Call it what it measures: routing concentration.

3. Shared routing backbone

15/20 top L42 experts are shared between Bible and Christian corpora. The shapes track closely — both corpora use the same routing backbone, with tradition-specific specialists layered on top.

8. Retractions

8.1 "Not-memorized-scripture" e164. Digit-density confound (pipeline strips Bible verse numbers; perfectly monotone deciles 0→16,448/M; zero-digit Christian control 4.9/M; ~29-expert cluster incl. e27, e68). Found by Claude Opus 5 review. Resolved by Exp 12: e164 is a digit detector (1,453× ratio).
8.2 Layer sandwich. REAP noise-floor artifact; inverts on frequencies; write-up mixed two Jaccard metrics. Found by Kimi K3 review.
8.3 Genesis 1:2 logit lens. Position 30 token is ' deep' not 'darkness' — the lens claim was based on a tokenization error.
8.4 Effective experts = 111. Pooling artifact (111→70 per-sample). The ordering survives; the absolute aggregate does not.
8.5 H3: routing concentration = predictability. Correlation is positive (+0.47), not negative. The 65 vs 111 gap is a window-length artifact.
